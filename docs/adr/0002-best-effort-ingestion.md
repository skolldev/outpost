# Best-effort ingestion

Outpost uses a bounded in-memory queue of references to ephemeral on-disk envelope spools for best-effort telemetry ingestion because it targets individuals and teams rather than very large telemetry volumes. Spooling bounds queued heap use independently of envelope size and lets the queue absorb deeper bursts; it is a capacity mechanism, not a durability guarantee. A full queue applies backpressure to Sentry SDKs, while malformed or unprocessable items are dropped so ingestion remains healthy. On graceful shutdown, Outpost stops accepting traffic and drains acknowledged telemetry for a bounded period before reporting any residual work. Crash durability, spool replay, and at-least-once delivery remain explicitly out of scope: acknowledged but unpersisted telemetry can still be lost when the process is killed, crashes, or exceeds the drain timeout, avoiding the deployment and operational complexity of a durable message broker.
