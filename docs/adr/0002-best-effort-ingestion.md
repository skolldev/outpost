# Best-effort ingestion

Outpost uses a bounded in-memory queue for best-effort telemetry ingestion because it targets individuals and teams rather than very large telemetry volumes. A full queue applies backpressure to Sentry SDKs, while malformed or unprocessable items are dropped so ingestion remains healthy. On graceful shutdown, Outpost stops accepting traffic and drains acknowledged telemetry for a bounded period before reporting any residual work. Crash durability and at-least-once delivery remain explicitly out of scope: acknowledged but unpersisted telemetry can still be lost when the process is killed, crashes, or exceeds the drain timeout, avoiding the deployment and operational complexity of a durable message broker.
